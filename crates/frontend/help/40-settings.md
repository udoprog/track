title: Settings

![Settings](icon:cog-6-tooth) **Settings** in the toolbar opens your
**Preferences**. Administrators also see the pages that apply to everyone.

## Preferences

- **Theme**: dark, light, or whatever the system uses.
- **Time zone**: the zone dates are shown in. Leave it empty to use the
  browser's.
- **Up next per page** and **Up next look-ahead**: how many items What's Next
  shows, and how far ahead it includes episodes that have not aired yet.
- **Specials in What's Next**: whether specials count as the next episode.
- **Language**: the language titles and overviews are shown in, when a
  translation exists.

## For administrators

- **Site**: the **Page title** shown in the toolbar and the browser tab.
- **Sync**: **Automatic sync**, how often it runs, and which languages are
  synced. See [Remotes and syncing](remotes).
- **Sources & dates**: the order sources are used in and what each provides,
  and the rules choosing which release and air dates count.
- **API keys**: the TheTVDB and TMDB keys. Without one, that source is not
  searched or synced.
- **Cloudflare Access**: signing in through Cloudflare Access.

## Your account

![Account](icon:user-circle) your login in the toolbar opens your account,
where you change your login, email and password, or sign out.
