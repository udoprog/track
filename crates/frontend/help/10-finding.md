title: Finding and adding shows and movies

Track only follows what is in your library. To add something, open
![Search](icon:magnifying-glass) **Search** in the toolbar, type a title and
press Enter.

Shows are searched on TMDB and TheTVDB, movies on TMDB. A source is only
searched once its API key is set under **Settings > API keys** (see
[Settings](settings)). Each result shows whether it is a
![Show](icon:tv) show or a ![Movie](icon:film) movie and which sources know
it. Narrow the results with the Shows and Movies chips:

```track
media-kind
```

Press ![Track](button:plus) on a result to add it. Track syncs it from its
sources and opens its page. A result you already follow says
![Tracked](icon:check) **Tracked** and opens the page you have.

## Tracking

Each show and movie page has a tracking button. Only tracked items appear in
**What's Next**, **Upcoming** and **Schedule** on the dashboard, and tracking
is per user: removing something from your list leaves it for everyone else.

```track
tracked
```

## The media list

![Media](icon:film) **Media** lists the library as a poster grid. Type in
**Filter** to find a title, in any language it is known by. **Sort by** orders
the list by title, release date or when you last watched it, and the
direction chip reverses it.

The ![Tracked](icon:eye) tracked chip switches between everything, what you
track and what you do not. The **Next** chip keeps only shows with a next
episode to watch: any, a regular one, or a special.

Select cards with their checkboxes to act on several at once: track or
untrack them, mark the next episode of each show, or mark movies watched. See
[Watching](watching).
