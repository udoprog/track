title: Users

Everyone on a track server shares its library of shows and movies, but each
user has their own tracking, watch history and preferences. Administrators
manage users from ![Users](icon:users) **Users** in the toolbar.

## Inviting someone

Press **Add user**, give a login, and optionally an email and a role, then
**Add user** again. Track shows a registration link for them in the
**Link** column: copy it and send it to them yourself, since track sends no
mail. The link works once and expires after a week, and it is only shown
this once.

![A newly added user, pending, with their registration link and the actions
for them](images/users-invite.webp)

They are **Pending** until they open the link and choose a password, and
**Active** after.

## Roles

A **Regular user** tracks and watches. An **Administrator** also sees the
settings that apply to everyone, the **Users** page, and can run or remove
tasks in the queue. Change a user's role with **Edit**.

## Managing access

Each user has these actions:

- **Edit** changes their login, email and role.
- **New link** makes a fresh link, which replaces any earlier one: to
  invite again after a link expired, or to let someone who forgot their
  password choose a new one.
- **Revoke link** stops a link from working.
- **Sign out everywhere** ends all their sessions.
- **Revoke access** signs them out and clears their password; only a new
  link lets them back in.
- **Delete** removes the user with their sessions and links. It cannot be
  undone.

Your own login, email and password are changed from your account; see
[Settings](settings).
